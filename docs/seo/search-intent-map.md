## Competitive gap and Recallly opportunities

Bulkmark targets broad management, export, thread, privacy, digest, comparison, and alternatives queries. Recallly should build authority around narrower product-backed strengths:

- reading rich saved X content inside the product while preserving the original source;
- category plus topic filtering rather than deep folder structures;
- multimodal AI enrichment for saved posts with available images;
- rediscovery workflows that combine search, metadata, and source context;
- accurate guides to X API history limitations and safe JSON export.

Future articles should extend these clusters deliberately. Comparison and alternative pages require separate research and must not be generated as thin variants.
